Hardware Engineering & GPU Acceleration

Supported Processors, SoCs & Device Models

Bobine compiles native binaries for both x86_64 (amd64) and ARM64 (aarch64) without emulation overhead, directly leveraging VA-API, NVDEC, V4L2, VideoToolbox, and MediaCodec hardware video decoding engines.

x86_64 Architecture (Intel & AMD Ryzen)

Built for 24/7 studio mini-PCs (fanless or whisper-quiet), desktop workstations, and laptops operated in closed-lid clamshell mode. Delivers 1080p60 and 4K Ultra HD hardware decoding under 8% CPU utilization.

  • Intel Low-Power & Appliances: Intel Processor N100, N200, N95, N97, Core i3-N305 (Alder Lake-N / Twin Lake) and Intel Celeron J4105, J4125, N5105 (Dell Wyse 5070 thin clients, Beelink Mini S12 Pro, Minisforum UN100, MeLE Quieter 4C). Intel QuickSync VA-API acceleration (iHD / i965).
  • Intel Core & Core Ultra: Intel Core Ultra 5 / 7 / 9 (Series 1 Meteor Lake & Series 2 Lunar Lake / Arrow Lake, Intel Arc GPU) and 8th–14th Gen Intel Core i3 / i5 / i7 / i9 (Intel NUC, ASUS NUC, HP EliteDesk 800 Mini, Lenovo ThinkCentre Tiny, Dell OptiPlex Micro).
  • AMD Ryzen (Zen 3, Zen 4, Zen 5): AMD Ryzen 5000, 6000, 7000, 8000 & Ryzen AI 300 (Ryzen 5 5560U, Ryzen 7 5800H, Ryzen 7 7840U/HS, Ryzen 7 8845HS — Beelink SER5 / SER7 / SER8, Minisforum UM780 XTX, Geekom A7 / A8, Framework Laptop 13 & 16). AMD Radeon VA-API acceleration (radeonsi).

ARM64 Architecture (Snapdragon, Apple Silicon & SBC)

Engineered for next-generation ultra-efficient ARM64 laptops, flagship tablets with USB-C DisplayPort Alt Mode video output, and single-board computers.

  • Qualcomm Snapdragon PC (Oryon): Snapdragon X Elite (X1E-80-100, X1E-84-100, X1E-78-100), Snapdragon X Plus (X1P-64-100, X1P-42-100), and Snapdragon 8cx Gen 3 (Lenovo ThinkPad T14s Gen 6, Yoga Slim 7x, Surface Laptop 7, ASUS Vivobook S 15, Dell XPS 13 9345).
  • Consumer Flagship & Tablet SoCs: Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 (SM8550-AB), Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 (SM8650-AB), Snapdragon 8 Elite (SM8750-AB), Snapdragon 8+ Gen 1, MediaTek Dimensity 9300+ / Kompanio, and Google Tensor G2 / G3 / G4 (Samsung Galaxy Tab S9 / S9+ / S9 Ultra, Xiaomi Pad 6S Pro / Pad 6 Max, OnePlus Pad, Google Books / ARM64 Chromebooks).
  • Apple Silicon, NVIDIA & SBCs: Apple M1, M2, M3, M4 (Mac mini, MacBook Air/Pro under Asahi Linux / VM or native macOS), NVIDIA DGX Spark (Grace Blackwell GB10) & Jetson Orin, Raspberry Pi 5 (Broadcom BCM2712), Orange Pi 5 & Radxa Rock 5B (Rockchip RK3588).
Security & Integrity

Unified OpenPGP Cryptographic Signature

Every repository index (InRelease, repomd.xml.asc, bobine.db.sig) and binary package is signed with the official Bobine key. Your package manager automatically rejects any tampered file.

Official Signing Key Fingerprint (SHA-1 / V4)

When prompted during your first APT, DNF, or Pacman installation, verify that the key fingerprint matches:

B77D 96D7 2F84 F36A CAA3 F872 9CBF 497D 829E E15A
Direct key inspection command: curl -fsSL https://packages.bobine.fit/bobine.gpg | gpg --show-keys

Distribution Technical Notes

  • Fedora & RPM Fusion (Full ffmpeg): Fedora ships ffmpeg-free by default without certain hardware decoders. Enable RPM Fusion and run sudo dnf swap ffmpeg-free ffmpeg --allowerasing for full codec support.
  • RHEL 9, AlmaLinux & Rocky Linux: Enable the epel-release repository first (sudo dnf install epel-release) so multimedia and WebKitGTK dependencies resolve cleanly.
  • In-App Updates: Bobine automatically detects whether it was installed via dpkg/apt, rpm/dnf, or pacman and applies updates using your native package format via Polkit.
Complete Ecosystem

Other Supported Operating Systems & Browsers

Android, Google Books & Android TV
